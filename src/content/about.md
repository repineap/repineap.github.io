# About

Software engineer on the data platform team at Betterment Holdings. Living in NJ, working in Manhattan.

Beyond that, someone who believes that data is the solution to most of our problems and a die-hard Bayesian.

The world is in a very transitional period so I find it important to do what I want and do it as well as I can.

Who knows what the world will be like in 5 years (or maybe in 6 months), but humans will continue to learn and create new things which I hope to contribute to.

As AI has become far more widely used, people have been given the opportunity to stop learning. We are able to offload the mental parts of anything to AI and it takes conscious effort to really fight that.

This website is meant to keep me accountable to keep working towards my goals and on my projects (and try to show off things I think are cool).

Would love to connect with anyone else who is interested in hockey, data science, programming, Rust, AI, step functions, and of course most importantly Greek food.
